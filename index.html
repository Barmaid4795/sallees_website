About Me

Tansi, Sallee Icy Schaerer nisihkason. Otipemisiwak niya.

My family comes from the Scott, Smith, Morrison, and Raymond lines, and their history is woven into everything I make. I'm from Alberta, and it's where my art, my design work, and my connection to community all grow from — I work across painting, digital design, and handmade craft, moving between fine art, client work, and pieces that carry my Indigenous identity and story.
