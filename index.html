About Me

Tansi, Sallee Icy Schaerer nisihkason. Otipemisiwak niya.

Hello, my name is Sallee Icy Schaerer and I am a member of the Metis Nation of Alberta. I work across painting, digital design, and handmade craft, moving between fine art, client work.

My family names:
