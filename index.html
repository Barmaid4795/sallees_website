About Me

Tansi, Sallee Icy Schaerer nisihkason. Otipemisiwak niya.

Hello, my name is Sallee Icy Schaerer and I am a member of the Métis Nation of Alberta.

Family, place, and practice

My family comes from the Scott, Smith, Morrison, and Raymond lines, and their history is woven into everything I make. I'm from Alberta, and it's where my art, my design work, and my connection to community all grow from.

  • Scott
  • Smith
  • Morrison
  • Raymond

I work across painting, digital design, and handmade craft — moving between fine art, client design work, and pieces that carry my Indigenous identity and story. Whether you're here for a commission, a collaboration, or a professional opportunity, I'm glad you're here.

Sallee Icy Schaerer is a Métis artist and designer whose work spans acrylic painting, mural and digital design, and handmade creations. Her practice moves fluidly between fine art, client work, and pieces rooted in her Indigenous identity and community.

Want to get in touch? Jump to the contact section below, or reach me directly at salleeschaerer@gmail.com.
